Shamir Secret Sharing

Instead of giving one person the master password, we split the AES-256 backup key into a polynomial equation given to 5 executives. To restore the database, 3 of the 5 executives must physically combine their pieces.

Network Speed

Regional Bloom Filters

Full database replication across the globe is expensive and slow. We push highly compressed bit-arrays (Scalable Bloom Filters) to Regional Replicas. Edge-servers instantly check if an agent is revoked in less than a millisecond.

Threshold Ceremony (Hardware Quorum)

Decoupled cryptography interface allowing seamless swaps to YubiKeys or KMS. No single "God Key" exists. Existential quorums require physical 3-of-5 validation, and total system wipes require the absolute maximum 5-of-5 quorum.
